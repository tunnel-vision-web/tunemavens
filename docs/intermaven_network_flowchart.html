KEY DATA & FINANCIAL STREAM WORKFLOWS
Step-by-Step Data Pipelines: Audio Ingestion, Sync Deals, Direct Fan Tipping & Social AI Studio
Page 2 • Pipeline Workflows
1. Audio Stem & Metadata Ingestion Pipeline
Step 1: Creator Uploads Audio Stems
Artist initiates upload on tunemavens.com. Frontend requests presigned S3 URL from FastAPI backend.
Step 2: Binary Stems Persisted to S3 / Cloudflare R2
High-definition audio stems (WAV/AIFF 24-bit) streamed directly to Object Storage.
Step 3: Metadata Indexing & ISRC Generation
FastAPI indexes title, splits, genre & ISRC code into MongoDB catalogs collection.
Step 4: Sync Clearance & Multi-Site Fanout
Catalog marked is_cleared_for_sync: true and synced to SyncMavens & TuneStream player.
2. Sync Licensing Match & Escrow Split Waterfall
Step 1: Music Supervisor Submits Brief
Supervisor posts project brief (budget, license type, genre) on syncmavens.com.
Step 2: AI Match Simulation & Pitch Submission
AI Match Engine scores catalog stems against brief. Creator approves pitch submission.
Step 3: E-Signature Execution & Stripe Escrow Deposit
Contract generated in contracts. Supervisor deposits license fee into Stripe Escrow.
Step 4: Automated Waterfall Payout Disbursed
Option 1: 30% Master / 25% Sync / 52.5% Writer; Option 2: 15% Agency Fee dispatches automatically.
3. Fan Lossless Streaming & Direct Tipping Engine
Step 1: Fan Listens on TuneStream
User streams lossless audio on tunestream.co web or native Capacitor app.
Step 2: Stream Event Logged in MongoDB
Playback event written to activity_events collection for real-time social graph recommendations.
Step 3: Fan Triggers One-Click Micro-Tip
Fan tips artist $5-$50 via Stripe payment widget directly from player interface.
Step 4: Immediate Creator Wallet Credit
Stripe Connect transfers funds directly to artist's linked bank account with 0% platform lockup.
4. Social AI Studio & EPK Marketing Pipeline
Step 1: Creator Prompt Input
Creator inputs visual concept or lyrics in Mother Platform intermaven.io.
Step 2: AI Multi-Format Generation
Gemini Nano generates cover art; Sora 2 renders 9:16 short vertical promotional video clips.
Step 3: Mother-EPK Auto-Hydration
Assets, catalog tracks & press stats compiled into interactive EPK grid link.
Step 4: CRM Marketing Campaign Dispatch
Resend API emails EPK press blast & push notifications to opted-in fans and industry leads.

Security & SSO Token Architecture:
Single Sign-On is handled by an OIDC / PKCE PyJWT token engine running at auth.py. Session cookies are wildcard-scoped to .tunemavens.com, allowing seamless cross-app auth across all 4 sites without re-authenticating.
Financial Auditability & Escrow Protection:
All licensing payouts (Option 1: 30/25/52.5 and Option 2: 15% agency fee) and direct fan tips are backed by Stripe Connect double-entry ledgers. Admin approval is required for payouts exceeding $10,000 to prevent fraud.
