## Key Flowchart Design Enhancements

### 1. Vertical Domain Column Alignment
The flowchart is aligned into **four vertical domain columns**, mapping every layer directly above and below its corresponding components:
- **Domain 1 (Mother Platform & SSO Core)**: `Creators & Artists` $\rightarrow$ `INTERMAVEN PORTAL` $\rightarrow$ `SSO Auth & Social AI` $\rightarrow$ `Mother-CMS Admin Console` $\rightarrow$ `MongoDB Cluster`.
- **Domain 2 (TuneMavens Marketplace)**: `Record Labels, DJs & Corp` $\rightarrow$ `TUNEMAVENS PORTAL` $\rightarrow$ `Catalog Stem Ingest` $\rightarrow$ `DSP Catalog & Distro Admin` $\rightarrow$ `MongoDB Cluster`.
- **Domain 3 (SyncMavens Licensing)**: `Music Supervisors` $\rightarrow$ `SYNCMAVENS PORTAL` $\rightarrow$ `AI Brief Match Engine` $\rightarrow$ `AI Governance & Ops Admin` $\rightarrow$ `AWS S3 Bucket`.
- **Domain 4 (TuneStream App & Tipping)**: `Fans & Listeners` $\rightarrow$ `TUNESTREAM APP` $\rightarrow$ `Player & Fan Tipping` $\rightarrow$ `Stripe Escrow & Payout Admin` $\rightarrow$ `Stripe Escrow Ledger`.

### 2. Zero-Crossing & Channel Routing Architecture
- **In-Domain Vertical Flows**: Primary data flows travel strictly down vertical column tracks without crossing any adjacent node boxes.
- **Shared SSO Token Channel**: Cross-domain authentication flows from `TuneMavens`, `SyncMavens`, and `TuneStream` route through a dedicated horizontal channel gutter at `Y=210` around node boxes directly into `SSO Auth`.
- **Stem Sync Pathway**: Stem metadata fanout routes horizontally between `Catalog Stem Ingest` and `AI Brief Match Engine` through clear channel gaps.
- **Admin Control Perimeter**: System Admin control pathways route around the outer right perimeter (`X=968`) directly into the Lane 4 Governance Panels.
