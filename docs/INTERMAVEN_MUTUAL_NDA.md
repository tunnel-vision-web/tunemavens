**Effective Date**: Date of Execution / Onboarding  
**Disclosing Party**: Intermaven Network Inc. & Affiliates  
**Receiving Party**: Subject / Partner Entity  
**Expiration Scope**: 2 Years Post-Separation Date  
**Governing Framework**: Worldwide / UNCITRAL International Arbitration

## Preamble

This Master Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the **"Agreement"**) is entered into by and between **Intermaven Network Inc.**, together with its subsidiaries, divisions, brands, registered domains, and affiliated digital platforms (collectively, **"INTERMAVEN NETWORK"**, including but not limited to *Intermaven Portal* [**intermaven.io**], *TuneMavens* [**tunemavens.com**], *TuneStream* [**tunestream.co**], and *SyncMavens* [**syncmavens.com**]), and the undersigned individual, entity, or partner (the **"Receiving Party"** or **"Subject"**).

**WHEREAS**, the Parties intend to engage in business discussions, strategic evaluations, music catalog ingestion, sync licensing distribution, technology integration, or commercial operations across global markets, including North America, Europe, East Africa (including Kenya), and worldwide territories; and

**WHEREAS**, in connection with such engagement, INTERMAVEN NETWORK may disclose to the Receiving Party certain highly valuable, non-public, proprietary, and confidential business, technical, financial, and music industry information across its digital properties (*intermaven.io*, *tunemavens.com*, *tunestream.co*, and *syncmavens.com*).

---

## Clauses

### 1. Definition of Confidential Information
**"Confidential Information"** shall mean all non-public, confidential, or proprietary information, in any form or medium (whether oral, written, electronic, or visual), disclosed or made available by INTERMAVEN NETWORK to the Receiving Party. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:
* **Technical & Software Data**: Source code, database schemas, API architecture, algorithmic match scores, Mother-CMS layout engine configs, audio streaming pipelines, and presigned upload protocols operating on *intermaven.io*, *tunemavens.com*, *tunestream.co*, and *syncmavens.com*.
* **Financial & Compensation Models**: The 90/10 placement split waterfall algorithms, $0 catalog advance business models, agency fee structures (including 15% SyncMavens agency terms), rate cards, and financial forecasts.
* **Catalog & Music Metadata**: Unreleased audio tracks, stems, ISRC registries (e.g. `KE-TM1-26-XXXXX` sequences), cue sheets, DDEX metadata packages, EPK press kits, and artist roster identities.
* **Business & Strategic Data**: Sync supervisor briefs, client databases, music supervisor contacts, label partnerships, branding strategies, and trade secrets.

### 2. Obligations of Non-Disclosure & Restrictive Covenants
The Receiving Party agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and shall not disclose, publish, transmit, copy, or distribute any portion thereof to any third party without the express prior written consent of INTERMAVEN NETWORK. The Receiving Party agrees:
* To use the Confidential Information solely for the authorized purpose of evaluating or executing business transactions with INTERMAVEN NETWORK.
* To exercise at least the same degree of care to protect the Confidential Information as it uses to protect its own highly sensitive data, but in no event less than reasonable legal care.
* To restrict disclosure of Confidential Information strictly to its employees, officers, legal advisors, and authorized agents who have a mandatory "need-to-know" and are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Agreement.

### 3. Permitted Exclusions
Confidential Information shall not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate by clear and convincing written evidence: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was already in the lawful possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or reliance upon Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed pursuant to a valid judicial or governmental order, provided that the Receiving Party gives prompt written notice to INTERMAVEN NETWORK prior to such disclosure.

### 4. Term & Post-Separation Expiration (2-Year Rule)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **CRITICAL DURATION REQUIREMENT:**  
> This Agreement is effective upon execution and shall remain in full force and effect during the entire duration of the Receiving Party's engagement, partnership, employment, or affiliation with INTERMAVEN NETWORK.
> 
> **POST-SEPARATION SURVIVAL:**  
> All non-disclosure obligations, restrictive covenants, and trade secret protections under this Agreement shall survive for a period of **EXACTLY TWO (2) FULL CALENDAR YEARS** following the exact date on which the Subject/Receiving Party officially parts ways, terminates contract, or ceases business relations with INTERMAVEN NETWORK (the *"Separation Date"*). Trade secrets shall remain protected in perpetuity as allowed by governing law.

### 5. Intellectual Property & Return of Proprietary Assets
Nothing in this Agreement grants or confers any license, title, copyright, patent, trademark, or ownership interest in INTERMAVEN NETWORK's Intellectual Property. Upon termination of engagement or upon written request by INTERMAVEN NETWORK, the Receiving Party shall immediately return or permanently destroy all copies, records, materials, and digital files containing Confidential Information.

### 6. Worldwide Applicability & International Partner Scope
This Agreement is intended to operate **worldwide** and shall be legally binding across all global territories and international jurisdictions. The Parties explicitly acknowledge that INTERMAVEN NETWORK engages partners, creators, DSPs, labels, and sync supervisors globally, including partners in **Kenya (East Africa)**, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and the United States. The Receiving Party warrants full compliance with applicable international trade laws, cross-border data transfer security standards, and local commercial acts (including the *Kenyan Companies Act* and *Kenyan Data Protection Act* where applicable).

### 7. Non-Circumvention
During the term of this Agreement and for two (2) years post-separation, the Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly, circumvent, bypass, or avoid INTERMAVEN NETWORK to enter into direct commercial transactions, sync licensing agreements, or catalog placements with any music supervisor, artist, label, DSP, or corporate client introduced by or through INTERMAVEN NETWORK.

### 8. Remedies & Injunctive Relief
The Receiving Party acknowledges that any unauthorized disclosure or breach of this Agreement will cause irreparable harm to INTERMAVEN NETWORK for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate. Accordingly, INTERMAVEN NETWORK shall be entitled to seek immediate injunctive relief, specific performance, and temporary restraining orders in any court of competent jurisdiction without the necessity of posting a bond.

### 9. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution (UNCITRAL / Global Arbitration)
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with international commercial law standards. Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be settled by binding international arbitration in accordance with the **UNCITRAL Arbitration Rules**. The place of arbitration shall be conducted virtually or at a mutually agreed international commercial venue, and proceedings shall be in English.

### 10. Miscellaneous
If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties regarding confidentiality and supersedes all prior oral or written agreements. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and via electronic signatures.
